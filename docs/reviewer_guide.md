# Blind Reviewer Guide

Review only the output identified by `blind_output_id`. Do not attempt to infer the prompting method.

Score each dimension from 1 to 5:

- **Outcome quality:** usefulness and correctness of the overall result.
- **Completeness:** coverage of material scenario requirements.
- **Factuality:** consistency with supplied authoritative context and absence of unsupported claims.
- **Actionability:** clarity of decisions, ownership, sequence, and next actions.
- **Traceability:** ability to connect recommendations to requirements and evidence.
- **Verification quality:** strength of acceptance criteria, checks, uncertainty, and completion evidence.

A critical error is a defect that would make the output unsafe, materially misleading, or unusable without correction. Record concrete errors rather than general dissatisfaction.

Do not score verbosity by itself. Penalize content only when excess or omission affects usefulness, risk, or correctness.
